Nordmark Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard


📊 Project Overview
This project presents a business-driven Power BI analytics solution developed for Nordmark Retail Group, a mid-sized omnichannel retailer operating through physical stores and e-commerce channels across Europe. The dashboard was designed to support supply chain managers, inventory planners, retail operations teams, e-commerce managers, and business analysts in making data-driven decisions.

The solution transforms raw operational data into actionable insights by monitoring inventory performance, sales activity, replenishment operations, and stock movement across multiple locations and product categories.

Rather than focusing solely on reporting metrics, the project addresses real-world supply chain challenges related to inventory availability, replenishment efficiency, stock optimization, and operational performance.

🎯 Business Objectives
The primary objectives of this project are to help stakeholders:

Reduce stockout incidents across stores and online channels
Improve product availability and inventory visibility
Identify high-performing products and categories
Detect excess inventory and overstock situations
Improve replenishment planning and inventory allocation
Monitor inventory movement across the supply chain
Support better coordination between stores and e-commerce operations
Improve inventory turnover and operational efficiency
Enable proactive decision-making instead of reactive responses
🏢 Business Background
Nordmark Retail Group operates through a multi-node supply chain network where inventory flows from suppliers to distribution centers and then to retail stores and online customers.

Key Business Challenges
Stockouts resulting in lost sales and reduced customer satisfaction
Excess inventory causing increased holding costs
Poor forecasting processes
Inventory misalignment between physical stores and e-commerce operations
Reactive inventory decisions instead of proactive planning

📌 Project Outcome
This project demonstrates how Power BI can transform operational supply chain data into a comprehensive decision-support solution.

By combining inventory analytics, sales monitoring, inventory movement tracking, and replenishment analysis within a single dashboard, the solution helps Nordmark Retail Group move from reactive operations toward proactive supply chain management.

The dashboard provides stakeholders with greater visibility into inventory performance, sales trends, stock risks, and operational efficiency, supporting better business decisions across the organization.
